Who We Are

Founded in 2020 by Ms. Devika Pakruthi in Visakhapatnam (Vizag), Andhra Pradesh, Adhoc Network Tech is an APIS-incubated (Andhra Pradesh Innovation Society) technology-driven enterprise. Originally launched as a platform for IAS and government job aspirants, it has evolved into a comprehensive skill development and product engineering company.

By integrating cutting-edge technology, industry-relevant curriculum, experiential internships, and real-time learning analytics, we deliver continuous career breakthroughs for students and modern solutions for businesses — recognized nationally by FICCI FLO and featured in HerZindagi's Womenpreneur Awards.

🎯

Our Vision
